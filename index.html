Razion – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026

Razion ("the game") is a mobile game published by MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information the game itself collects

Razion does not require an account. Your game progress, settings, upgrades, purchased items and local high scores are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

How Google uses this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown and lets you decline. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings screen. Where you decline, only non-personalised ads (or no ads) are shown.

Purchasing the AD-FREE option removes all advertisements from the game.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (the AD-FREE option and PREMIUM CASH packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google Play provides us only with a purchase token used to grant the item on your device. Payments are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Data we receive

Beyond aggregated, non-identifying advertising and purchase statistics provided by Google, we do not receive personal data from the game. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and we do not operate any server that the game connects to.

5. Children

Razion is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, such requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google via the links above; you can also contact us and we will help where we can.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this address, with the effective date shown at the top.
